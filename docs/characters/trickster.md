# 🃏 Fae Trickster
*Born from the folklore of a city and its villages, stories of dark creatures that tempt children to lie. It exists to cause chaos and deceive, yet has a soft spot for the less fortunate.*

**Role:** Diver · **HP** 210 · **Armor** 15 · **Base damage** 34 · **Speed** 1.05 · **Attacks** 1.3/s

**Passive · Sucker Punch**
Your first swipe on an enemy **silences** them for 1s, unless they hit you first. Ready again after 8s with no damage between you. A ✦ over an enemy's head (only you see it) means they're ready.

**LMB · Swipe**
A quick swipe for 100% base damage, no knockback (weapon: a copper hoe, for now). While you're inside your Trick Room, every swipe that lands on an enemy takes 1s off Play Rough's cooldown.

**1 · Play Rough** (5s)
Lunge a step forward and slash the 3-block cone in front of you, **twice**, 0.2s apart, 75% base damage each, with no knockback. Each lunge stops in front of anyone in the way. A stun or silence after the first slash loses the second.

**2 · Trick Room** (14s)
Dash 7 blocks ahead and drop an 11×11 room where you land, for 10s. It reaches 2 blocks up and 2 down; its floor is a magenta and purple checkerboard on the ground at the bottom of the room, whoever is inside sees its checkered walls, and outline rings stacked up to 3 blocks high mark its edges from outside. Its edges glow through walls for you only. While it's magenta it's yours: inside it you get **+15% speed**, and **SHIFT + move** teleports you 6 blocks that way, every 0.5s (never through walls, never out of the room).
Press **2** again while you're inside the room (once per room): dash 4 blocks where you're facing, and the room moves to where you land and turns **topsy-turvy** for the rest of its 10s: its floor turns light blue and it's against your enemies instead. You lose the speed and the teleports, but every enemy who's been in it for 0.3s has inverted controls, until 0.3s after they step back out (no damage).

**3 · Confuse Ray** (14s)
Fire a slow fae beam (16 blocks). The first enemy it hits takes 20% base damage + 90% **spell power** and is **confused for 3s**: they turn to face their closest teammate, run at them and attack them with their basic attack over and over, 50% faster but at half damage (their ability 1 is half damage too; a crossbow is drawn and fired for them). Their keys do nothing meanwhile. No teammate nearby: the closest neutral (a camp, a boss), then the closest enemy, and only then you. Nobody in range at all: they're simply stunned. Anything they kill meanwhile counts as your kill.

**F · Switcheroo** (ultimate)
Point at an enemy within 15 blocks: you both float up and slowly fly to each other's spot, passing each other on the way (1 to 2.5s). Meanwhile neither of you can act or be targeted (damage over time still ticks). You land facing each other. Nobody there, or they can't be moved, and you keep your charge.
